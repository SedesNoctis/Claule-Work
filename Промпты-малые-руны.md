# Промпты для ChatGPT: малые (соединительные) руны

К каждому промпту лучше приложить ваш лист основных рун (19 глифов), чтобы стиль совпал.
Присылайте результат листом — я разрежу, как основные руны, и сделаю тёмную (Ink) и светящуюся (Glow) версии.

---

## 1. Лист из 8 малых рун

```
Context: Avir are a fictional race of tall, winged, feathered humanoid apex predators with their own ritual magic. Their ritualists draw circles of concentric rings on the floor in chalk; every ring is divided into slots, each slot holds a main rune (the attached sheet shows the main runes - hand-drawn ink glyphs with organic curls, leaf, feather and claw shapes). Between the slots, smaller connecting runes link the main runes into one pattern. These connecting runes are purely ornamental.

Task: draw a sheet of 8 MINOR CONNECTING RUNES in exactly the same hand and ink style as the attached main runes, but clearly subordinate to them:
- simpler, fewer strokes (2-4 strokes each), about half the visual weight of a main rune;
- tall and narrow (they sit in the gap between two slots of a ring, roughly 1:2 width to height), vertically symmetric or nearly so;
- they must read as "links" or "knots" that join things: stitches, binding knots, small feather quills, claw ticks, beads on a thread, a closed eye, a seed, a twist;
- each of the 8 clearly different from the others and from the main runes; no letters of any real alphabet, no numbers;
- must stay readable when scaled down to 24 pixels tall: no hairlines, no fine detail, stroke weight the same as in the main runes.

Format: pure black ink (#18100A) on a pure white background, no texture, no shading, no glow, no frame. Arrange them in one row of 8, evenly spaced, each centered in its own invisible square cell with generous empty margin, nothing touching. Image 2048x512.
```

## 2. Печать пустого слота

```
Same Avir ritual style as the attached runes. Draw ONE seal that marks an EMPTY slot in a ritual ring: a small closed mark meaning "nothing written here, the line is closed" - for example a ring of dots, a closed knot, or a small sealed circle with a mark in the middle. Lighter and calmer than any rune, perfectly radially symmetric, readable at 24 pixels. Pure black ink (#18100A) on pure white background, no shading, centered with wide margins. Image 1024x1024.
```

## 3. (по желанию) Печать центра круга

```
Same Avir ritual style as the attached runes. Draw the central seal of a ritual circle - the spot where the ritualist stands. Radially symmetric, made of 6 or 8 repeated small feather/claw strokes around a small open circle, like a compass or a flower of talons. Calm, balanced, a little more elaborate than a rune but still bold and simple enough to read at 64 pixels. Pure black ink (#18100A) on pure white, no shading, centered with wide margins. Image 1024x1024.
```

## Советы
- Если ChatGPT рисует слишком тонко — допишите: "make the strokes as thick as in the attached runes".
- Если рисует буквы — допишите: "these are not letters, avoid any resemblance to Latin, Cyrillic, Greek, runic or Arabic letters".
- Нужно 8 малых рун (по две на кольцо в большом круге, чередуются). Если понравится больше — пришлите больше, добавлю.

## 4. Рамка-печать вокруг основной руны (слот)
Один кружок, в который вписывается основная руна. Показывает игроку, куда ставить руну; пустой — это и есть пустой слот.
```
Same Avir ritual style as the attached runes (hand-drawn black ink calligraphy, organic curls, feather and claw shapes). Draw ONE SLOT FRAME: a small, slightly irregular hand-drawn ring that encloses a rune - not a perfect geometric circle, the line swells and thins like a brush stroke, and at 4 (or 6) points around it the line pushes OUTWARD into short sharp thorns / claw tips pointing away from the centre, evenly spaced, like a seal or a tiny compass. The inside is completely EMPTY - a rune will be placed there later - so the open middle must take up about 70% of the width. The ring line is about as thick as the strokes of the attached runes; it must stay readable when the whole frame is 48 pixels wide. Perfectly centred, roughly symmetric, no letters, no shading.
Pure black ink (#18100A) on pure white background, wide empty margins. Image 1024x1024.
```
Варианты, если захотите разнообразия: «with 4 thorns» и «with 6 thorns» — для малого и большого круга; или «with 3 thorns» для секций по 3 руны.
