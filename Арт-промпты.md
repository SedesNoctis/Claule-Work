# Арт для FIXES-ZK — промпты (вторая версия)

Уже готово и разложено:
- сферы управления;
- глифы рун Крови и Покрова.

Иконки переделываем **в старом стиле мода**: пергамент, чернила, золото, рамки. Это как Anchor, Rend, Wide reach и другие. Порядок рамок тот же, что раньше:
- **круглая рамка** — активные способности (кнопки);
- **ромбовидная рамка** — пути (эмблемы для выбора).

Пути рисуются символами, а не сценками того, что делает способность.

Осталось 4 генерации: 2 листа иконок архитектов, 1 лист Страйдеров, 1 лист эффектов.

---

## 1. Архитекты: эмблемы путей и активки (лист 3×3)

```
A single sprite sheet of 9 game icons arranged in a 3x3 grid, each icon in its own equal square cell with generous empty space between cells, plain transparent background, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels.
Unified style for all 9: hand-inked on aged parchment, dark iron-gall ink linework with gold leaf accents, engraved medieval-alchemical look, Avir telekinetic-psychic magic, one bold central symbol per icon, strong silhouette readable at 32 px, flat front view, same line weight, same palette (sepia ink, gold, a little deep red) and same lighting across the whole sheet.
Icons 1 to 5 are the emblems of the five paths of an architect, each inside a diamond-shaped frame. Icons 6 to 9 are active abilities, each inside a thin circular rune frame.
Top row:
1. Archconstruct - a gear wheel with a small glowing crystal orb at its hub, three tiny floating spheres circling it.
2. Master of Snares - a rune circle drawn on the ground, a single thread of light stretched across it like a tripwire.
3. Lord of Subspace - a small starry rift held between two cupped hands, a satchel strap curling around them.
Middle row:
4. Compulsive Creator - a hammer and a chisel crossed over an anvil, small sparks rising like a crown.
5. Energy Architect - a winding river of light flowing around a crystal sphere held in silver claws.
6. Soulmark - a chained hand joined by a thin glowing thread to a small mechanical mask.
Bottom row:
7. Subspace veil - a round shimmering veil around a small figure, arrows stopping against it and sinking in.
8. Veil discharge - the veil bursting outward in rings of light, the swallowed arrows thrown back out.
9. Obsession - three small workbenches side by side with a tool floating over each, lifted by telekinetic glow.
```

## 2. Архитекты: ловушки, купол и пламя (лист 5×2)

```
A single sprite sheet of 10 game ability icons arranged in a 5x2 grid, each icon in its own equal square cell with generous empty space between cells, plain transparent background, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels.
Unified style for all 10: hand-inked on aged parchment, dark iron-gall ink linework with gold leaf accents, engraved medieval-alchemical look, Avir telekinetic-psychic magic, one bold central symbol per icon, strong silhouette readable at 32 px, flat front view, same line weight, same palette (sepia ink, gold, a little deep red) and same lighting across the whole sheet. Every icon sits inside the same thin circular rune frame.
Top row:

3. Fire snare - a rune circle with a tongue of golden flame rising from its centre.
4. Frost snare - a rune circle with sharp ice shards rising from its centre.
5. Blast snare - a rune circle bursting in a plain round blast of smoke and stones.
Bottom row:
6. Flash snare - a rune circle with a blinding eight-pointed star and sound rings around it.
7. Energy dome - a half-dome of light standing over a small patch of ground.
8. Golden flame - a column of golden fire falling from above onto the ground.
9. Silver flame - a column of pale silver moonfire falling from above onto the ground.
```

## 3. Страйдеры: пути и выстрелы (лист 5×2)

Верхний ряд — эмблемы путей лука (символы, а не стрела с эффектом). Нижний ряд — большие выстрелы на 18 камнях.

```
A single sprite sheet of 10 game icons arranged in a 5x2 grid, each icon in its own equal square cell with generous empty space between cells, plain transparent background, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels.
Unified style for all 10: hand-inked on aged parchment, dark iron-gall ink linework with gold leaf accents, engraved medieval-alchemical look, Avir telekinetic-psychic magic, one bold central symbol per icon, strong silhouette readable at 32 px, flat front view, same line weight, same palette (sepia ink, gold, a little deep red) and same lighting across the whole sheet.
Every arrow on this sheet is an ordinary arrow: a single arrowhead at the front and feather fletching at the back - never two arrowheads.
Top row = the five paths of a longbow, each inside a diamond-shaped frame:
1. Hunt - a predator's eye above three claw marks.
2. Storm - a storm cloud crest with a single bolt beneath it.
3. Flame - a phoenix feather burning at its tip.
4. Moon - a thin crescent moon with a taut bowstring across it.
5. Gravity - a falling star pulled into a small ring that bends around it.
Bottom row = the five great shots, each inside a thin circular rune frame:
6. Judgement - a vertical blade of light piercing a ring of runes above a kneeling figure.
7. Sky strike - a bolt of lightning falling from a cloud onto the ground.
8. Fire rain - many burning arrows falling from above in slanted lines.
9. Moon arrow - one arrow crossing the full disc of the moon, leaving a long trail of light.
10. Gravity knot - several small figures pulled together into a knot of rings.
```

## 4. Эффекты (лист 3×3)

Без изменений с прошлого раза. Это то, что видно в игре при срабатывании, а не иконки. Только белое и светло-серое, цвет игра даст сама.

```
A sprite sheet of nine separate magical effects, arranged in a grid of 3 columns and 3 rows, each effect alone in its own equal square cell with clear empty space between cells. All seen from above, each centred in its cell.

Pure white and very light grey only, no other colours, so they can be tinted later. Soft glowing edges, no outlines, no shadows, no text, no labels, no grid lines.

Top row, left to right:
1. A burst of sharp ice crystals and frost spikes radiating from the centre.
2. A blinding flash: a bright round core with long thin rays like a star.
3. A single thin shockwave ring, bright and crisp, fading toward the inside.
Middle row, left to right:
4. A splash of liquid bursting outward from the centre in drops and streaks.
5. A soft drifting wisp of smoke or shadow, shapeless and feathery.
6. A round rune disc for a trap: a double ring with small marks, and an inward spiral of four arcs in the middle.
Bottom row, left to right:
7. The same rune disc, with a jagged bolt in the middle instead of the spiral.
8. The same rune disc, with a sharp diamond flame in the middle.
9. The same rune disc, with an eight-pointed star of rays in the middle.

Transparent background.
```
