# Промпты магии (копятся здесь; автору — по запросу)

## Жизнь

### Заросли корней (2–3 варианта, одним листом)
> A game asset sheet of three variations side by side, RimWorld top-down art style, transparent background, each 256×256: a low tangled thicket of living roots and thorny vines bursting out of the ground, gnarled brown-green roots arching over each other, sharp thorns, a few pale leaves, clumps of soil thrown up at the edges; each variation a different tangle so they do not repeat when placed side by side. Crisp painted shapes, a thin dark outline, soft even shading, no light direction.

### Мёртвая земля (наложение на почву)
> A seamless top-down ground overlay for a game, RimWorld style, transparent background, 256×256: withered grey grass and dry dead stalks lying flat over the soil, faded ash-grey and pale stone colours, a few cracks, patchy so the ground beneath shows through at the edges; no light direction, soft even shading.

### Оболочка из коры и мха (вокруг пешки)
> A single game effect sprite, top-down, transparent background, 512×512: a translucent rounded shell around an empty centre, woven of living bark plates and soft green moss, small leaves at the seams, faint green-gold light in the gaps between the plates; the centre fully transparent where the pawn stands; the shell itself semi-transparent so the pawn shows through. No light direction.

### Купол из ветвей (зональный оберег)
> A single top-down game effect sprite, transparent background, 1024×1024: a round dome seen from above woven of translucent living branches and vines, spiralling from the rim to a small open crown at the centre, fresh leaves and moss along the weave, a soft green-gold glow where branches cross; semi-transparent throughout so what is beneath stays visible. No light direction.

## Иконки заклинаний — рамки (референс: Референс-ромб-линариек.png — иконки тактик Линариек, Textures/UI/Avir/Herald/Tactic_*.png)

### Лист рамок: ромб, щит, круг (рамка + подложка)
> A game UI asset sheet, transparent background, 1536×1024, two rows of three, each item centred in its own 512×512 cell with generous empty margin. Same style as the attached reference icons: a polished antique-gold rim with fine engraved double edges, warm highlights and a thin dark outline, and small pointed gold DIAMOND studs set on the rim at its key points; painted, crisp, no text.
> TOP ROW - the RIMS ONLY, the inside completely transparent: (1) a DIAMOND (a square turned on its corner, its corners softly rounded) with a diamond stud at each of its four corners; (2) a SHIELD - a heater shield with a gently curved top and a softly pointed bottom, a diamond stud at the top centre, one at each upper corner and one at the bottom point; (3) a CIRCLE - a round rim exactly like the reference, a diamond stud at top, bottom, left and right. All three share one rim thickness and one gold, so they read as one set; the empty middle of each takes about 75% of its width - an emblem will be placed inside later. Each must stay readable at 64 pixels wide.
> BOTTOM ROW - the PLATES: the same three shapes filled flat with a pale, almost white parchment (very light grey-beige, a faint paper grain, an even soft vignette at the edge), exactly filling the inside of the rims above, no rim, no studs, clean edges - the game tints them with colour.
