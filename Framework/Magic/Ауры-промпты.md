# Ауры: промпты для картинок

Технические требования одни на все (картинка ложится кольцом вокруг пешки и медленно вращается):
- 1024×1024 PNG, **прозрачный фон** (альфа), вид строго сверху.
- Круглое кольцо, вписанное в квадрат с полем ~2%; центр пустой или почти прозрачный (видна земля и пешка).
- Радиально равномерно — без «верха» и «низа», без надписей, без рун-букв, без силуэтов людей.
- Свечение изнутри кольца мягко гаснет к центру (≈ треть радиуса), снаружи кольца — резкий край.
- Сохранять: `Textures/Things/Mote/Avir/Magic/Aura_<Имя>.png` (Fire, Water, Earth, Air; позже — сочетания наслоением и перекрасом).

## Fire — Aura_Fire
Top-down magical fire aura ring on a fully transparent background, perfectly circular, radially symmetric, seen from directly above. A thin bright ring of living flame at the outer edge: licking orange-gold tongues of fire curling inward, white-hot core line, embers and tiny sparks scattered along it. Inside the ring a soft warm amber glow fading to full transparency toward the centre; the centre is empty. Painterly fantasy game VFX, high contrast, no smoke, no text, no symbols, no characters, 1024x1024, PNG with alpha.

## Water — Aura_Water
Top-down magical water aura ring on a fully transparent background, perfectly circular, radially symmetric, seen from directly above. A thin ring of swirling clear blue water at the outer edge, like a standing wave running in a circle: bright white foam crests, small droplets thrown off it, caustic light ripples. Inside the ring a faint cool blue mist fading to full transparency toward the centre; the centre is empty. Painterly fantasy game VFX, no text, no symbols, no characters, 1024x1024, PNG with alpha.

## Earth — Aura_Earth
Top-down magical earth aura ring on a fully transparent background, perfectly circular, radially symmetric, seen from directly above. A ring of cracked raised stone and churned soil at the outer edge: jagged rock shards, pebbles and dust rising from the cracks, a faint warm ochre glow in the fissures. Inside the ring a thin haze of brown dust fading to full transparency toward the centre; the centre is empty. Painterly fantasy game VFX, no text, no runes, no characters, 1024x1024, PNG with alpha.

## Air — Aura_Air
Top-down magical wind aura ring on a fully transparent background, perfectly circular, radially symmetric, seen from directly above. A whirling ring of pale wind at the outer edge: thin white-blue streaks spiralling in one direction, wisps of cloud and a few leaves and specks of dust caught in the current. Inside the ring barely visible swirling air fading to full transparency toward the centre; the centre is empty. Painterly fantasy game VFX, light and airy, no text, no symbols, no characters, 1024x1024, PNG with alpha.

## Основа для перекраса — Aura_Base
Top-down neutral magical aura ring on a fully transparent background, perfectly circular, radially symmetric, seen from directly above. Pure white and light grey only: a bright thin energy ring at the outer edge with soft irregular glow and fine sparkles, a faint glow inside fading to full transparency toward the centre; the centre is empty. Designed to be tinted in-game. No colour, no text, no symbols, no characters, 1024x1024, PNG with alpha.

Сочетания потом собираем из двух родительских, как купола: магма — Fire + Earth, пар — Fire + Water, марево — Fire + Air, топь — Water + Earth, буря — Water + Air (плюс синие молнии), пыль — Earth + Air.
